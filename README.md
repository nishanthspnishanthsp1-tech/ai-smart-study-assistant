# AI Smart Study Assistant

## 🎓 Project Overview

AI Smart Study Assistant is an AI-powered personalised exam preparation platform designed to help college students prepare for semester examinations more effectively.

The system helps students analyse question papers, understand questions based on their marks, generate structured exam-ready answers, revise important topics, and organise their preparation in one platform.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many college students struggle to prepare effectively for semester examinations because study materials are scattered across notes, PDFs and different online resources.

Students also face difficulty in understanding how much information should be written for different mark questions and identifying repeated or important questions during revision.

AI Smart Study Assistant aims to provide a single platform that makes exam preparation faster, easier and more personalised.

## 💡 Solution

The system follows a simple workflow:

**Question Paper → AI Analysis → Exam-Ready Answer → Revision**

Students can upload their question papers, analyse the questions using AI, generate answers according to the required marks, change the answer language, listen to answers using text-to-speech, save important answers and revise efficiently.

## 🤖 AI Capabilities

The AI assistant is designed to:

1. Understand the question.
2. Identify the topic and context.
3. Understand the required marks.
4. Decide the appropriate answer depth.
5. Generate a structured exam-ready answer.
6. Support multilingual learning.
7. Identify repeated or similar questions.
8. Provide study and revision assistance.
